## Measurements and fit
Reference jeans: Sugar Cane 1947 (SC41947), size 34, which fit well. Published one-wash flat specs: waist ~16.5" across (~33" around), front rise ~11.75", thigh ~12.25", leg opening ~9" (charts vary ±0.5"). Likes a looser fit than that: high rise, roomy thigh, straight leg with no taper and a wide opening. Happy to go bigger in the waist (up to a few inches) and belt it to get thigh room.

## Taste
Jeans: men's cut, looser fit with a high rise, straight leg, no taper. Loves super faded, light-wash vintage denim. Already owns repro denim (Sugar Cane 1947), so appreciates heritage/workwear denim.

## Buying style
Ships to Brooklyn, NY (11225), US. Jeans budget: under $70. Wants clean pieces: small holes are fine, but no rips, tears or stains.
